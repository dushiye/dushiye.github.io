Sustained Orbital Motion in Nanoscale Stator–Rotor Architectures

First-author project

  • Developed a nanoscale stator–rotor architecture for sustained orbital motion under circularly polarized plane-wave illumination and identified helicity–polarity coupling as the underlying force mechanism.
  • Derived a symmetry-constrained optical-force law distinguishing nonconservative CPL-driven cyclic work from conservative LP responses, and validated the principle across ring, enclosing-shell, and eccentric-stator architectures.
  • Combined FDTD force calculations, multi-objective optimization, photothermal modeling, and Brownian dynamics to develop a confined Ag-nanoshell nanorotor with reduced optical heating.

Maxwell Descriptors for Physics-Aware Nanophotonic Design

Co-first-author project

  • Co-developed geometry-agnostic Maxwell descriptors that encode electric and magnetic multipolar channels and their interference within a shared electromagnetic representation across heterogeneous nanostructures.
  • Built a structure–descriptor–spectrum learning framework that improves spectral prediction, cross-geometry transferability, and physical interpretability over geometry-specific representations.
  • Applied the framework to physics-aware inverse design of a two-state rod–shell photothermal switch with strongly separated optical resonances and a recoverable electromagnetic mechanism.

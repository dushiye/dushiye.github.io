About

Hi, I am Shiye Du (杜是也), a chemistry undergraduate at Westlake University. My research interests lie at the interface of nanophotonics, mechanics, and physics-aware AI-assisted computational design.

Since January 2025, I have been working with Prof. Hongyu Chen at Westlake University, with my research co-supervised by Prof. Mouhong Lin at Zhejiang University. My current work focuses on optical-force-driven nanoscale actuation, including sustained orbital motion in nanoscale stator–rotor systems, as well as physics-aware inverse design and reconfigurable plasmonic nanostructures.

During my senior year, I will conduct research with Prof. Hanqing Jiang at Westlake University on acoustic materials and metamaterials, extending my interests from nanophotonics toward wave- and structure-based mechanics.

Featured Research

Sustained Orbital Motion Driven by Circularly Polarized Light in Nanoscale Stator–Rotor Architectures

Jan. 2025 – Present
ManuscriptFirst author
Prof. Hongyu Chen's Lab, Westlake University; co-supervised by Prof. Mouhong Lin, Zhejiang University
MechanismHelicity–Polarity Coupling

We develop a nanoscale stator–rotor architecture that converts normally incident, spatially uniform circularly polarized light into persistent orbital motion of an achiral spherical rotor. An off-center rotor displacement defines an instantaneous in-plane polarity, and optical helicity couples to this polarity to generate a position-following tangential force.
